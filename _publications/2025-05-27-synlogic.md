This paper presents SYNLOGIC, a data synthesis framework and dataset covering 35 logical reasoning tasks. The generated examples support rule-based verification, controlled difficulty, and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards. The work also shows that mixing SYNLOGIC with mathematics and coding data improves training efficiency and reasoning generalization.
