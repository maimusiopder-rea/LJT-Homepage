The paper identifies two bottlenecks in chart understanding: the vision encoder's ability to encode chart information and the language model's ability to extract relevant encoded information. It develops enhanced CLIP variants and chart-specific instruction tuning, showing that sharper visual representations improve both learning efficiency and downstream VLM performance.
