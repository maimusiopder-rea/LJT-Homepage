I am a second-year Ph.D. candidate at [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/hkust-nlp), advised by Professor [Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. in Automation (IEEE Honor Class) from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) in 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with an emphasis on:

- Agentic LLMs and long-horizon reasoning
- Logical reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Vision-language models and perception bottlenecks
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability

I am currently a research intern at **Apple MLR** in Cupertino, mentored by [Yizhe Zhang](https://dreasysnail.github.io/). Previously, I interned at MiniMax, where I contributed to large-scale logical reasoning data synthesis and training for the M1 and M2.x models, as well as Tencent WXG and Shanghai AI Lab.

I am broadly interested in building trustworthy and capable reasoning systems.
